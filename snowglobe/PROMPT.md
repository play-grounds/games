# Snow Globe: the brief

The fifth game was chosen from the brainstorm of twenty-five ideas ranked out of 100. The pitch, recorded as written:

---

**Snow Globe** (82/100). A wintery village in a shakeable globe: snow with physics that settles on roofs, sledging, ice skating, aurora at night, a shake fills the air with snow. Natural sibling to Bottleship.

---

The build expanded that into: a glass globe on a carved base on a mantelpiece by a fire, a village with lit cottages, a church, a Christmas tree with blinking lights, a frozen pond with skaters, a sledging hill with kids riding down and trudging back, a horse-drawn sleigh, a snowman and a dog, snowflakes that fall with wind and settle into a depth grid over every surface, shaking that tilts the scene and throws the settled snow back into the air, snowballs on click, aurora curtains and stars at night, and a music box tune.
