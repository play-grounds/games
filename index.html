PLAY-GROUNDS GAMES

Small browser games. Open one and play, nothing to install.

Siegeworks

A voxel diorama of a medieval capital under siege. Watch the town go about its day, then breach the gatehouse, drop a boulder, or summon the dragon. Works on desktop and phone.

VOXEL · SIEGE · SANDBOX · THREE.JS
PLAY

Bottleship

A galleon rides a living voxel sea sealed inside a glass bottle on a study desk. Waves with buoyancy physics, dolphins, a sea serpent, a lighthouse, and storms that roll through with lightning.

VOXEL · OCEAN · PHYSICS · THREE.JS
PLAY

Clockwork Orrery

A brass solar system under glass in a study at night. Eight voxel planets with weather and moons, a comet, an asteroid belt and a ticking gear train, lit by a sun that casts real eclipse shadows.

VOXEL · PLANETS · CLOCKWORK · THREE.JS
PLAY

Railway Model

A tabletop model railway. A steam express and a diesel freight run through a tunnel and over a viaduct, past a station, level crossings, a fairground and a farm. Throw the switches, pull the whistle, or open the throttle until something derails.

VOXEL · TRAINS · SWITCHES · THREE.JS
PLAY

Snow Globe

A winter village under glass on a mantelpiece. Snow falls and settles on roofs and pines, skaters circle the pond, kids sledge the hill, a sleigh trots the lane. Shake it and the snow fills the air while the music box plays.

VOXEL · SNOW · COSY · THREE.JS
PLAY

Zen Garden

A dry garden you rake with your finger. Furrows follow your strokes, rings form around the stones you set, koi gather where you tap the pond, and the maple turns through the seasons. Wind, water, chimes and a singing bowl.

VOXEL · MEDITATIVE · SOUND · THREE.JS
