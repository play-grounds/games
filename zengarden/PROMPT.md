# Zen Garden: the brief

The sixth game was chosen from the brainstorm of twenty-five ideas ranked out of 100. The pitch, recorded as written:

---

**Zen Garden** (75/100). Rake sand with your finger, place stones, a koi pond, seasons change the maple. Meditative, sound-led.

---

The build expanded that into: a walled dry garden seen from a veranda, gravel as a live heightfield whose furrows follow your strokes, stones that drop in and raise rings, a smoothing tool, a koi pond with fish that gather where you tap, lily pads, a heron and a frog, a maple that runs through blossom, summer green, red leaf fall and bare snowy branches, a bamboo fountain that fills and clacks, a wind chime, a stone lantern that glows at night, a moon gate onto distant hills, and a sound bed of wind and water with gravel scrape, chimes, a singing bowl and a temple bell on the hour.
