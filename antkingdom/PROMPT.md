# Ant Kingdom: the brief

The seventh game was chosen from the brainstorm of twenty-five ideas ranked out of 100. The pitch, recorded as written:

---

**Ant Kingdom** (90/100). Cross-section of an ant colony in a glass terrarium: tunnels dug in real time, foraging trails, a rival colony raid, rain flooding tunnels, an anteater snout. Emergent life, sandbox digging with your finger.

---

The build expanded that into: a glass-fronted tank on a stand whose soil is a live cell grid, digger ants that carve tunnels and open chambers and haul the spoil up to build the mound, foragers that path over flow fields to crumbs and lay pheromone on the way home so trails emerge, a queen who lays eggs from the food store, eggs that grow into larvae and ants with roles chosen by what the colony needs, soldiers, cave-ins where soil is undercut, rain that floods through a water automaton and drowns ants who cannot get out, a rival red colony that raids for eggs, an anteater whose tongue follows the tunnels down, and tools to dig, fill, drop sugar and pour water with your finger.
