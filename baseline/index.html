HOW TO PLAY

  1. Serve: click (or tap) once to toss the ball, then click again when it reaches the top of the toss.
  2. Rally: your player runs to the ball by herself. Click when the ball reaches her. The meter at the bottom shows the ball coming in: swing when the mark hits the gold.
  3. Aim: where you click matters. Left side of the screen sends it left, right sends it right. Click high for a lob, low for a drop shot.
  4. Keyboard: Space swings and serves, arrow keys aim.

BASELINE

Voxel tennis. Your player runs to the ball on her own. You choose the moment and the direction.

How to play. Click once to toss, click again at the top of the toss to serve. In a rally your player runs to the ball on her own: click when the ball reaches her, using the meter at the bottom. Click left or right of the screen to aim, high for a lob, low for a drop shot. Space and the arrow keys do the same.
