# Baseline: the brief

The eighth game came from a second brainstorm, this time for something popular and in a different style: women's tennis. Fifteen ideas were ranked out of 100 and the flagship was chosen. The pitch, recorded as written:

---

**Baseline** (93/100). A playable voxel women's tennis game: rally with timing and aim, full scoring, serve, volley, lobs, drop shots, opponents with distinct styles (big server, counterpuncher, net rusher), a season across four fictional Slams. The obvious flagship.

---

The first release covers a single match: real court geometry and scoring with tiebreaks, a serve you toss and strike, ball physics with gravity, drag, bounce and net cord on hard, clay or grass, auto-positioning with timing-and-aim control that works one-handed on a phone, four fictional opponents with their own styles, foot speed and error models, three difficulty levels, a reacting crowd, and an umpire who calls the score.
