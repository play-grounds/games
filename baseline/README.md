# Baseline

A playable voxel women's tennis game, in a single HTML file. Play it at https://play-grounds.github.io/games/baseline/

- The brief that produced the game is in [PROMPT.md](PROMPT.md).
- Built with [Three.js](https://threejs.org/) r128 from cdnjs; the stadium, the players, the ball physics and the umpire's voice (the browser's own speech synthesis) are all generated in the browser. The players and tournament are fictional.

## How to play

- **Serve:** click or tap once to toss, then again at the top of the toss.
- **Rally:** your player runs to the ball herself. Swing when the mark on the meter reaches the gold band.
- **Aim:** where you click or tap matters. Left sends the ball left, right sends it right, the top band of the screen is a lob. On desktop a low click is a drop shot; on phones use the DROP button.
- **Keyboard:** Space swings and serves, arrow keys aim.
