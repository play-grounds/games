# Clockwork Orrery: the brief

The third game was chosen from a brainstorm of twenty-five ideas ranked out of 100. The winning pitch, recorded as written:

---

**Clockwork Orrery** (92/100). A brass solar system inside a glass dome on a desk: voxel planets with weather, moons, comets, an eclipse you can trigger, gears turning below. Totally different mood, very showcase-friendly.

---

The build expanded that into: eight procedurally surfaced planets with moons and rings, Earth's drifting clouds and night-side lightning, Mars dust storms, a Kepler-paced comet, an asteroid belt, a visible gear train with escapement, balance wheel and winding key, a real point-light sun that casts eclipse shadows, a grand-conjunction button, hover labels with facts, four cinematic camera paths, and a ticking clockwork soundscape.
