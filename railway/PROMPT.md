# Railway Model: the brief

The fourth game was chosen from the same brainstorm of twenty-five ideas ranked out of 100. The pitch, recorded as written:

---

**Railway Model** (83/100). A tabletop model railway: tunnels through hills, a viaduct, level crossings, a tiny town with a fair, trains you can switch and derail. Nostalgia plus interaction.

---

The build expanded that into: a mainline loop with a branch line and a goods loop joined by switches, terrain that conforms to the track with cuttings, a tunnel bore and viaduct piers, a steam express that stops at the station while passengers board, a diesel freight, block signalling so the trains never collide, two level crossings with barriers and waiting cars, a fairground with a ferris wheel and carousel, a farm with a windmill, a town with a church that chimes the hour, a day/night cycle with lit windows and headlamps, a footplate camera, and derailments with tumbling wagons if the throttle is pushed too far on a curve.
